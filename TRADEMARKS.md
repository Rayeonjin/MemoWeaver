# MemoWeaver name and brand usage policy (draft for owner approval)

`MemoWeaver`, its Korean name, and the distinctive mosaic-brain icon identify
the original MemoWeaver note application. They are separate from the copyright
license of the project's source code.

- Forks and derivative apps may reuse licensed source code under the applicable
  terms, but should adopt a distinguishable product name and branding.
- Do not present a modified app as an official MemoWeaver release or imply
  approval, sponsorship, partnership, or affiliation without permission.
- Accurate, clearly differentiated phrases such as "based on MemoWeaver" or
  "contains code from the MemoWeaver project" are welcome for attribution.
- The software license is not a blanket license to use MemoWeaver as a
  derivative app's product name or to reuse its logo as the new product icon.
- A source fork may identify itself as a fork of MemoWeaver in a factual,
  non-confusing description, provided it makes its unofficial status clear.

This policy expresses the owner's intended permitted uses. It does not itself
prove registered trademark rights or prohibit legally permitted nominative
references. Trademark protection depends on applicable law and existing rights.
Before claiming registration or exclusivity, search relevant databases, e.g.
KIPRIS in Korea, and consider professional trademark advice for software.

For permissions contact the original repository maintainer through GitHub.
